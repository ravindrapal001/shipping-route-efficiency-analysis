# 🍬 Nassau Candy Distributor – Factory-to-Customer Shipping Route Analysis

### End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Microsoft Excel | Business Intelligence | Dashboard Development

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the factory-to-customer distribution network of **Nassau Candy Distributor** using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to transform structured sales and distribution data into meaningful business insights by examining sales performance, factory contribution, product performance, customer behaviour, geographic distribution, shipping modes and estimated distance patterns.

Using Excel's data preparation, data modelling, analytical and visualization capabilities, I developed a comprehensive analysis workbook containing KPI calculations, PivotTables, PivotCharts, a final dashboard, business insights and actionable recommendations.

The project demonstrates how raw business data can be transformed into structured analysis to support better business understanding and data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Evaluate overall sales and gross profit performance.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing factories.
- Analyse product contribution and customer purchasing patterns.
- Understand geographic sales distribution across states and cities.
- Identify important factory-to-customer route combinations.
- Examine shipping-mode preferences and performance.
- Analyse estimated distance patterns across regions and shipping modes.
- Translate analytical findings into meaningful business insights and recommendations.

### Major Findings

**1. Factory Contribution**

- Lot's O' Nuts is the leading factory by sales.
- It contributes approximately 53.98% of total sales.
- Lot's O' Nuts and Wicked Choccy's together contribute approximately 92.93% of total sales.

**2. Geographic Performance**

- California is the leading state by sales.
- California contributes approximately 20.11% of total sales.
- California, New York and Texas together account for approximately 40.97% of total sales.

**3. Shipping Mode Performance**

- Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping mode.
- It accounts for approximately 59.91% of distinct orders.
- It contributes approximately 59.88% of total sales.

**4. Distance Analysis**

- The 1,500 km or more estimated distance band contributes approximately 60.68% of total sales.
- The Atlantic region records the highest average estimated distance among the analysed regions.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the project includes a dedicated Business Recommendations worksheet.

Key recommendations include:

1. **Factory Performance:** Review operational dependency on the leading factories and investigate differences in factory profitability.

2. **Geographic Expansion:** Prioritise major sales-contributing states while evaluating potential opportunities in lower-contribution markets.

3. **Shipping Mode Planning:** Monitor Standard Class demand and evaluate shipping-mode performance against customer requirements and actual transportation expenses.

4. **Route Analysis:** Review high-sales, long-distance route combinations to identify opportunities for further distribution-network investigation.

5. **Logistics Data Collection:** Collect actual delivery dates, transportation expenses, road distances and delivery-status information for more reliable logistics performance analysis.

6. **Performance Monitoring:** Use regular KPI monitoring to evaluate changes in factory, product, geographic and shipping-mode performance.

## ⚠️ Data Limitations & Analytical Considerations

To ensure responsible interpretation of the results, the following limitations should be considered:

- The distance analysis uses estimated geographic distances and should not be interpreted as verified road or actual delivery distances.
- Actual delivery time and on-time delivery performance cannot be established from the available data.
- Transportation and shipping expenses are not independently established by the available dataset.
- Recorded gross profit should not be interpreted as net profit.
- The available analysis is primarily descriptive and does not establish causal relationships or prove operational efficiency improvements.

Additional operational and logistics data would be required for a more comprehensive evaluation of actual delivery performance and route efficiency.
